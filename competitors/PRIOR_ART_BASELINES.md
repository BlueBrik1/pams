# Published techniques applicable as competitors for exact second-order masked ML-KEM ciphertext verification

**Scope.** This file covers only the literature needed to build performance competitors. The novelty question was settled in Phase XII (`validation/NOVELTY_REVIEW.md`, classification B).

**Sources.** Every entry was read from the full text available locally:
- `refs/`: bos2021, coron2023, danvers2022, danvers2023, x2x, sh2024, heinz2022, hw2022;
- `~/lwe_agent_handoff/phase2/ext/priorart/papers/`: fritzmann2022, azouaoui2023, coron2023dil, coron2024rej, cassiers2021trans, ranney2026, berthet2025, bronchain2022.

Gadget counts and randomness in the last column are for the hardware realization built here. They are measured from the generated RTL (`competitors/models/cgen.py`, numbers in BENCHMARKS.md). Every realization uses 3 shares and HPC2, with 3 fresh bits per gadget per cycle and one coefficient per cycle.

## 2. Why `bos` is the strongest realization of the Bos / Azouaoui family considered
- **Setup.** Let x_0' = (x_0 − a_c) mod q (share-local). Then s = x_0' + x_1 + x_2 ∈ [0, 3q − 3] < 2^14, and x' = (x − a_c) mod q ∈ {s, s − q, s − 2q}.
- **Predicate.** [x' < W_c] = [s < W] ∨ [q ≤ s < q + W] ∨ [2q ≤ s < 2q + W]. The windows are disjoint and each is a difference of two nested indicators, so this equals [s < W] ⊕ [s < q] ⊕ [s < q + W] ⊕ [s < 2q] ⊕ [s < 2q + W].
  - The XOR of five masked bits is linear.
  - Each threshold comparison is a fused ripple comparator: one HPC2 per bit, sharing one carry chain.
- **Cost.** 12 CSA majorities + 12 carries + 5 × 13 comparator steps = 89 kernel gadgets (+2 accumulator).
- **Literal Bos (T1)** needs two A2B_q. Each is ≥ the 14-bit integer sum plus a reduction, and the reduction needs two comparisons with q and 2q plus a masked subtraction of 0/q/2q. That is ≥ 2 × (24 + 2 × 13 + 26) ≈ 150 gadgets.
- **Literal Azouaoui (T2)** needs one A2B_q plus SecLeq, ≈ 24 + 26 + 26 + 13 ≈ 89 or more, the same order as `bos`.
- **No cheaper form was found.** Removing the wrap requires either reduction mod q (more gadgets) or a modulus switch, and the modulus switch turns the construction into Simple / HOCompress (T4/T6) or PAMS.

## 3. Generic engineering techniques applied (to the competitors **and** to PAMS)
| id | technique | provenance | applies to |
|---|---|---|---|
| j | **Just-in-time CSA.** A ripple adder over 3 Boolean-shared single-domain operands needs bit k only when the carry c_k arrives (stage ≈ 2k). Delaying the operand bits y_i[k] costs 1 FF per share bit and cycle. Delaying the 3-share S_k and T_k costs 6. | Standard retiming of operands toward their consumer. The share-locality of y_i[k] is what makes it cheaper. | every ripple design: base, hoc, bos, PAMS |
| p | **One extra share-wise pipeline stage after the constant multiplier.** Both frozen designs have their critical path through the multiplier plus the offset / shift (Phase XI STA). | Standard pipelining. | base, hoc, PAMS (bos has no multiplier) |
| bk | **Brent–Kung carry-only prefix tree** for the carry into bit t | X2X (T9) adder architecture; carry-only prefix from SH2024 (T10) | Simple family (base, hoc). PAMS's comparator needs every intermediate lt bit chained, so a prefix comparator was already rejected in Phase XI (≈ 99 vs 38 gadgets; CANDIDATES.md of Phase XI). |
