# Competitors considered, optimizations tried, and decisions

Each name is an RTL file in `rtl/`, generated by `models/cgen.py`. Numbers are synthesis medians (BENCHMARKS.md §1), or routed medians where marked (§2). "Fundamental" means the cost follows from the algorithm (gadget count, lane width); "RTL quality" means it follows from implementation choices that any design can adopt.

## A. Bos-style exact interval comparison (Bos 2021 / Azouaoui 2023 family)
**Strongest form found: `bos`** (PRIOR_ART_BASELINES.md §2):
- one share-local anchor, (x0 − a_c) mod q;
- a 14-bit integer A2B of three 12-bit shares with no modular reduction;
- the mod-q wrap folded into 5 public-threshold comparators combined by XOR, with one shared carry chain.

**Variants and results:**

| step | design | area | delay | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first implementation | `bos` | 28,604 | 1.642 | 91 gadgets / 273 rnd bits |
| optimization: JIT CSA | `bos_j` | 26,843 | 1.633 | −6.2 % area |

- No front-end pipelining: there is no share-wise multiplier to pipeline.
- **Diagnosis: the cost is fundamental.**
  - The wrap is the cost: three windows need five comparator chains, 65 of its 89 kernel gadgets.
  - Removing the wrap needs either a mod-q reduction (more gadgets) or a modulus switch, and the switch turns it into Simple / HOCompress, or into PAMS.
  - It has the fewest combinational cells (no multiplier), but its gadget area dominates.
- **Decision:** rejected from the finalists. bos_j is ≥ 19 % larger than every Simple-family variant (largest: hoc, 22,583 µm²) and 35 % larger than base_j, at equal or worse delay.

## B. Coron-style exact compression / comparison
**HOCompress precision (α = 14), `hoc`:**
- the Simple datapath at 14 fractional bits, a 25-bit lane; exact with A = 0;
- this is also the precision of the published D'Anvers 2023 code for 3 shares.

| step | design | area | delay | routed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| first implementation | `hoc` | 22,583 | 1.773 | — |
| + JIT + 1 stage | `hoc_jp` | 20,948 | 1.438 | 1.573 ns / 23,230 µm² |
| + JIT + 2 stages | `hoc_jq` | 21,820 | 1.116 | — |

- **Diagnosis:** the same structure as Simple, one bit wider; it is dominated by the corresponding base variant at every depth.
- **Coron arithmetic zero tests / candidate products (exact per coefficient):** rejected by the measured lower bound of ≥ 33,500 µm² (BENCHMARKS.md §4). The batched forms are probabilistic, so they are excluded by contract.

## C. Modern conversion-enhanced (X2X / SH2024 adder architecture)
**Brent–Kung carry-only prefix tree into bit t with HPC2 gadgets: `base_bk`.**

| step | design | area | delay | gadgets / rnd | latency |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first implementation | `base_bk` | 20,731 | 1.715 | 75 / 225 | 17 (vs 31) |
| + 1 stage | `base_bkp` | 21,019 | 1.512 | 75 / 225 | 18 |

- **Effect:** it removes most alignment registers (711 FFs versus 1,565), but adds 18 gadgets (+3,250 µm² of gadget area) and 54 random bits per cycle.
- **Diagnosis:** at one coefficient per cycle, a prefix carry trades cheap registers for expensive gadgets.
  - After the JIT CSA, the ripple is cheaper still: base_j 19,915 µm² versus base_bk 20,731 µm².
  - Its only win is latency (17 cycles), which the contract does not reward.
- **Decision:** not a finalist. It is dominated by base_j in area, and by base_jp / base_p in delay at the same depth.
- **X2X DOM / t-NI gadgets and half-cycle paths:** not adopted (FAIRNESS_AUDIT.md §3).

## D. Improved conventional baseline (re-examination of `base.v`)
| inspected overhead | finding | action | result |
|---|---|---|---|
| excessive registers | **yes.** ≈ 62 % of base.v's top-level (non-gadget) area is flip-flops (1,565 × 4.52 µm² of 11,318 µm²), most of them operand-alignment registers. The 3-share S_k / T_k wait ≈ 2k cycles for the ripple carry, but the single-domain bits y_i[k] they come from need only 1 FF per share bit. | **j: just-in-time CSA** | base_j: −7.8 % area (1,171 vs 1,565 FFs); delay unchanged |
| critical path | the share-wise 24-bit constant multiplier + mode shift + offset in one stage | **p:** register after the multiplier; **q:** also split the multiplier | base_p 1.388 ns (−15.8 %); base_jq 1.057 ns (−35.9 %) at 21,097 µm² |
| unnecessary conversions | none: one power-of-two A2B, no mod-q conversion | — | — |
| overwide intermediate values | t = 13 is the minimum exact precision for Simple (t = 12 has no exact offset; re-verified); 24 bits are needed for d = 11 | — | — |
| inefficient carry structures | ripple (minimal gadgets) versus Brent–Kung (minimal latency) | evaluated as C | ripple + JIT wins |
| redundant masked operations | none. The JIT boundary bit initially recomputed T_t, which was a redundant gadget **in my variant**; it now reuses the early T_t, so gadget counts equal base.v. | — | 57 gadgets in every base variant |
| unnecessary control logic | control is a shift-register pipeline (≈ 4 × latency FFs), identical in all designs | — | — |
| poor synthesis mapping | five mappings: spread ≤ 0.2 %; no anomaly | — | — |
| mode-dependent carry taps instead of the mode shift | needs the ripple up to bit 20 for d = 4 (7 more gadgets) | not built (derivation; Phase XI also rejected it) | — |

**Strongest baseline variants:**
- **base_j:** smallest, 19,915 µm² / 1.646 ns;
- **base_jp:** 20,048 / 1.457;
- **base_p:** 21,940 / 1.388;
- **base_jq:** fastest, 21,097 / 1.057; routed 23,290 µm² / 1.222 ns / 43,694 µm² die.

Together these form the competitor Pareto front.

## E. Best combination of published techniques
- **base_jq** combines:
  - Simple with the optimized t = 13 / A = 1 offset (Fritzmann 2022, D'Anvers 2023, Phase XI exhaustive offset);
  - the carry-free zero test and early OR tree (Phase XI baseline);
  - the JIT operand alignment;
  - a two-stage pipelined constant multiplier.
- The Brent–Kung carry (X2X) and the 14-bit HOCompress precision (CGMZ23) were tried in combination and are each dominated, so they are not part of it.
- **Provenance of the generic techniques:** standard retiming and pipelining. No published masked-verifier paper uses them in this form; they are engineering choices.
- **Strongest competitor overall:** base_jq for delay; base_j for area. No competitor is better than another on both, except where dominated.

## Fairness counterparts: the same generic optimizations applied to PAMS
| design | area | delay | routed | note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pams (frozen) | 16,998 | 1.487 | 1.613 ns / 18,704 µm² | |
| pams_j | 15,812 | 1.565 | — | −7.0 % area; mapping noise in delay (1.493–1.565) |
| pams_p | 17,349 | 1.306 | — | critical path moves to PAMS's public window logic |
| pams_jp | 16,207 | 1.234 | 1.385 / 17,844 | |
| pams_q, pams_jq | 18,133 / 16,997 | 1.212 / 1.242 | — | still limited by public window logic |
| **pams_jpr** | 16,237 | 1.163 | 1.248 / 17,903 | r: public logic split across the existing stages (no added latency; public data only) |
| **pams_jqr** | 17,022 | 1.033 | 1.162 / 18,706 | |

- **r is PAMS-specific but is the same kind of change as p:** pipelining public logic. It does not change the PAMS algorithm, its masked kernel, or its gadget count. The baseline has no equivalent need: its public offset logic is trivial and never critical.
- **Not done:**
  - deeper pipelining (+3 and beyond), for both families;
  - any change to the PAMS decision mechanism.

## Rejected without building
| idea | reason |
|---|---|
| probabilistic comparisons (D'Anvers 2022 / 2023 hybrid, GF; CGMZ23 batched) | not exact (contract) |
| X2X lower-order first SecADD layer (2-share adder for x0 + x1) | a 2-share sub-circuit spanning domains 0 and 1 is outside 3-domain trivial PINI composition; it would need a new proof and would apply to PAMS's identical A2B structure equally |
| HPC3 / DOM / other gadget families | contract fixes the gadget (HPC2); any change applies to both lanes (FAIRNESS_AUDIT.md §3) |
| per-d dedicated lanes | contract requires per-coefficient d; four lanes are strictly larger |
